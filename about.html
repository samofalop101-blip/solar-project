Our Story

Built on Trust, Powered by Purpose

B&M Solar and Inverter Ltd. was founded in 2015 with one clear mission: to give every Nigerian access to clean, affordable, and reliable electricity,regardless of the state of the national grid.

What started as a small team of two engineers in Lagos has grown into a nationwide operation with over 500 completed installations, a team of certified technicians, and a reputation built entirely on word-of-mouth and client satisfaction.

  • 🎯

    Our Mission

    To make solar energy accessible, reliable, and affordable for every Nigerian home and business.

  • 🌟

    Our Vision

  • 🤝
