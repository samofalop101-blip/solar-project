Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does installation take? +
Most residential installations are completed within 1–2 working days. Larger commercial systems may take 3–5 days depending on system size and site conditions.
Do I need to be on the national grid? +
No. We design both grid-tied systems (which work with PHCN) and fully off-grid systems that operate completely independently of the national grid.
What warranty do I get? +
Solar panels carry a 20–25 year manufacturer performance warranty. Inverters and batteries carry 2–10 years depending on the brand. Our installation work carries a 12-month labour warranty.
Can you install in any Nigerian state? +
We currently serve Lagos, Ogun, Abuja (FCT), Rivers, Oyo, and Edo states. We're rapidly expanding. Contact us for your location and we'll confirm availability.
Do you offer payment plans? +
Yes. We partner with select financial institutions to offer instalment payment plans for qualifying customers. Ask our team for details during your consultation.
How much can I save on electricity? +
Most households see electricity costs drop by 80–100%. On average, our systems pay for themselves within 2–4 years through savings on PHCN bills and diesel generator costs.
